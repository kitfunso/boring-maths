This is a site-level ranking demotion by Google, not a technical fault.

4. What we know, believe, and have not tested

Know: a site-wide ranking loss on Google web search from 2026-10-16, with the index intact and no technical or manual cause.

Believe: Google re-scored the whole site with a site-level quality signal. The profile fits: 181 templated calculators across unrelated niches (pottery, CNC, ovulation, UK tax, Singapore CPF), about 5 referring domains, AdSense, and clusters shipped in batches. The cliff came 8 days after the September spam update finished, which targets scaled content.

Not tested: which signal, and whether it reverses by itself. Google does not report site-level classifiers anywhere.
