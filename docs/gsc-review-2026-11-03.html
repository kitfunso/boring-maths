One thing changes the read on old numbers. Before the drop, desktop had 14,940 impressions and 1 click in two weeks, and mobile had 3,679 impressions and 11 clicks. Most of those impressions were probably rank trackers, so the 99% fall overstates the loss. Real Google visits fell about 60% at the cliff, and Search Console and Analytics agree on that figure.

Two Analytics bugs, fixed

Shipped in the same pull request as this report, on Keith's "ship".

  • Bots counted as visitors. A 3-second timer loaded tracking for anything that opened a page. Tracking now loads on the first real interaction.
  • calculator_used meant nothing. The event fired from page load in 156 calculators, or a stub swallowed it, depending on timing. It claimed calculation_completed: true with no input touched. It now fires once when a visitor touches a control in the widget, from one place, and covers every calculator.

Checks: type check 0 errors, lint clean, 6 new tests pass, and a planted timer fails the idle test. Full suite 1,395 passed before the last test was added. Build passes 240 pages and all eight SEO gates. In Chromium on the production build, an idle visitor sent 0 Analytics requests in 6 seconds, mouse movement loaded tracking with no use event, and typing sent one event with the slug.

tests/seo/gates.test.ts fails to parse on this PC. It passes on CI at the current master, 20 of 20. The cause is a CRLF line ending after the shebang in Windows checkouts of two scripts, and this change does not touch them.

After the deploy, Analytics sessions will step down. That is bots leaving, and traffic will not have changed. Not tested: whether the Singapore bots interact. Read Analytics a week after the deploy to find out.
