The research round's headline: the UK YMYL head terms stay backlink-locked, but a non-YMYL UK admin flank (student loans, statutory pay, childcare, redundancy) and the machining cluster are wide open at our authority level.

Findings

  1. www host leak (new this window). www.boring-math.com serves HTTP 200 instead of redirecting; uk-mortgage-affordability is now indexed under www (760 impr, pos 88.3) and apex (446 impr, pos 84.0). Canonical points to apex; Google surfaces www anyway. Correction (post-deploy): a _redirects host rule was tried and is a documented no-op — Cloudflare Pages does not support domain-level redirects. The real fix is Keith-gated and one click: CF dashboard → Rules → Redirect Rules → template "Redirect from WWW to Root" (301). Verify with curl -sI https://www.boring-math.com/ (expect 301).
  2. Seasonal fade, not ranking loss. party-drink 12 → 7 clicks, graduation-party 7 → 4 (graduation season over); positions held or improved (event-seating 21.9 → 19.0). The events cluster still earns ~40% of site clicks.
  3. UK money cluster shrinking. uk-capital-gains −468 impr, inheritance-tax −164 vs Jul-18, positions unchanged 76–84. Confirms authority-bound; Phase 4 backlinks (open since Feb) still the only lever for the head terms.
  4. New striking-distance assets: raise-calculator (2,544 impr, pos 13.7), conference-room (2,565, pos 14.6), cooking-time (3,290, pos 26.4) — the three biggest legitimate impression pools.
  5. discount-calculator is noise: +6,284 impr in ten days to 12.8k at pos 13.9 with 0 clicks. Google answers "X% off $Y" inline. Do not invest.
